ASSISTANT (17)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
**FaultTrace** is a maintenance knowledge app for plant-floor technicians at Cardinal Precision, a contract CNC/assembly manufacturer.

**The problem:** Years of work orders sit in a CMMS (and paper binders), but when a machine fails at 2 a.m., that history is hard to find. The same fault was written many ways — *spindle drift*, *spndl drift*, *SPIN-DRFT*, *axis wander* — so keyword search returns nothing or noise. Senior techs who “just know” the fix are retiring. On a bottleneck cell, that wasted hour stops the line.

**The use case:** A technician types a symptom or fault code on a tablet at the machine. FaultTrace finds similar past work orders with semantic search, summarizes what fixed it before, and cites the work-order IDs. If nothing reliable matches, it says so instead of inventing a procedure. Close-outs are structured so the knowledge base improves. Supervisors see recurring faults and downtime for the weekly meeting.

**In one line:** FaultTrace turns buried, messy maintenance history into a cited answer in under two minutes — so the floor doesn’t start from zero every time.
