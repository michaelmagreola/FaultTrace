Floor PPE requirements

Linked plant standard — not generated by FaultTrace. Wear the PPE listed for the area you are entering before troubleshooting.

Machining / CNC floor (minimum)

  • Safety glasses with side shields
  • Steel-toe or composite-toe footwear
  • Hearing protection in posted high-noise zones
  • Cut-resistant gloves when handling tooling or chips (remove gloves near rotating spindles)
  • No loose jewelry; hair/net restraint as posted

Assembly / conveyor areas

  • Safety glasses
  • Closed-toe footwear (steel-toe where posted)
  • High-visibility vest in forklift aisles

Before energizing equipment

Confirm LOTO is cleared by authorized personnel and guards are in place. See LOTO-CNC for isolation rules.
