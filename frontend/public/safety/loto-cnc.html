Lockout/Tagout — CNC cells

This page is a linked controlled procedure. FaultTrace never generates lockout steps. Follow only the approved plant document.

Scope

Applies to Haas, Okuma, and other CNC assets in Machining cells before any work that could energize the spindle, axis drives, coolant, or chip conveyor.

Required sequence (summary)

  1. Notify affected personnel and obtain work permit if required.
  2. Shut down using the normal equipment stop sequence.
  3. Isolate electrical disconnect and apply personal lock and tag.
  4. Isolate pneumatic/hydraulic energy as listed on the asset LOTO placard.
  5. Verify zero energy with the approved test method on the placard.
  6. Perform maintenance. Remove locks only after tools are clear and guards restored.

References

Plant EHS manual §4 · OSHA 1910.147 · Asset-specific placard on each CNC cell.
